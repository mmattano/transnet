Databases
---------

The network is assembled from these datases:
`KEGG <https://www.kegg.jp/>`_ (reactions, compounds, EC numbers, pathways,
signaling relations), `UniProt <https://www.uniprot.org/>`_ (proteins, EC
annotation, cross-references), `BRENDA <https://www.brenda-enzymes.org/>`_
(allosteric activators and inhibitors, kinetic constants),
`STRING <https://string-db.org/>`_ (protein-protein interactions),
`ChIP-Atlas <https://chip-atlas.org/>`_ (transcription factor to target gene),
`Ensembl <https://www.ensembl.org/>`_ (transcript models and identifiers),
`Reactome <https://reactome.org/>`_ (pathway annotation and over-representation)
and `HMDB <https://hmdb.ca/>`_ (metabolite annotation).
